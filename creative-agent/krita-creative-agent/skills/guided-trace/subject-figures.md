# Guiding figures

**What to guide**
- The figure's height in heads (about 7 to 8 for an adult), with marks for the head, chest, hips, knees and feet.
- A gesture line through the spine, then the shoulder and hip lines: their tilt carries the pose.
- The silhouette from the reference (`from_reference`, `detail: 'simple'`): the outer shape of the body and its
  clothing. Give the figure `shape: {points}` on its body (label 1) and on the ground beside it (label 0); a crowd is one
  shape per figure.
- Where the feet meet the ground, on the ground plane of the scene's perspective. A figure further back stands higher
  and is smaller. Heads of standing figures on level ground sit near the eye level when the viewer stands too.
- Hands and face only as placement marks: their detail is painted, not guided.
- Lit faces and rim light as light shapes when they are the brightest areas.

**Common errors**
- Heads too big, legs too short: count the heads.
- Feet floating above the ground, or figures that ignore the eye level.
- A stiff, symmetrical stance where the reference has a tilt in the shoulders and hips.
- Guiding every fold of the clothing: a few lines for the silhouette are enough.
