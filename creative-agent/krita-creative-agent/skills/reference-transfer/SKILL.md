# Transfer a reference

For faithful reference-assisted reconstruction, start with paint_from_reference: it locally samples an attached reference and plans coarse coverage, fine detail and optional focal refinement as small dabs. It is underpainting: it gets composition, values and colour right but cannot make edges, thin lines or distinct materials, and running it again only re-rolls the same dabs. Use it once for the whole picture, then only for a missing region; the host refuses it in the second half of the plan. When the reference is the whole picture (the canvas's proportions), map all of it onto the whole canvas; the host lays a toned ground in its colours first, and under a time allowance widens the steps so the job fits, then says so in the result. Realism comes from refining on top, not repainting: clipped layers for light, shadow and each material, and paint_accents; after a whole-picture underpainting the host allows at most 12 small marks a call unless the artist asks for hand-painted work. Blurring a dab texture makes it blurry, not realistic. Stage two, crispness, is paint_accents: name the area (the whole picture, a box, a selection), the kinds (lights, glints, darks, thin lines) and how many; the host compares the reference with the canvas and, where the canvas is softer, duller or paler, adjusts the canvas's own paint with Krita's filters (Dodge and a bloom for lights, Burn for darks, High Pass for lost detail) as one Accents group. Silhouettes come from the paint, never from outlines. paint_reference_edges paints paired edge strokes that look scratchy on painterly references: not for finishing. Where dab or mosaic texture remains between edges (hair, fabric, skin, sky, water, road), use paint_reference_surface with a Color Smudge blender such as k) Blender Basic: it smudges the paint already there along the reference's form flow in the smooth areas, on its own layer, then repaints the region's edges crisp; it replaces blur, which only makes dabs blurry. Busy areas (lit windows, reflections, snow, fur) are left for paint_accents. On a painted canvas every transfer, edges and surface included, is refused past 2x enlargement (accents are exempt) (target over source): a goal image much smaller than the canvas cannot sharpen hair or a face. Attach a closer reference of that area, or stop refining it there and ask the artist. After the underpainting a chat gets one whole-canvas softening pass (a smudge paint_pass or a blur filter over 60% of the canvas); soften later only one element or region. For a face or a hand late in the plan, attach a close-up reference of it if one exists and run paint_from_reference on just that feature (at most 3% of the canvas, so one face or hand per call), then paint_accents there; a focal patch is allowed at any step. paint_from_reference paints the whole job (coarse, fine and focal passes) in one call; call continue_reference_painting with its job_id only if a job was stopped or interrupted. Use list_reference_paintings to recover job IDs after restart. Choose source/target rectangles with matching aspect ratios and name focal source regions for faces/hands. A part reference (part_of) is see-through outside the one area to follow; a transfer from it paints only that area, in the place it has in the original. A crop is a close-up of bounds of the original. When the context lists close-ups (an element image or reference aligned to its place in the goal, confirmed by the artist), keep giving detail transfers their source in goal pixels: one whose source lies inside a close-up's goal box samples the sharper close-up instead, and the result says so. Until the artist confirms a close-up (they are asked beside the run), transfers there sample the goal; never wait for the answer. Close-ups that could not be placed are looks to follow, not sources. This is reference-assisted transfer, not independently drawn anatomy. For original invention or freely composed work, use authored painting tools instead.
