# Transfer a reference

For faithful reference-assisted reconstruction, start with paint_from_reference: it locally samples an attached reference and plans coarse coverage, fine detail and optional focal refinement as small dabs. It is underpainting: it gets composition, values and colour right but cannot make edges, thin lines or distinct materials, and running it again only re-rolls the same dabs. Use it once for the whole picture, then only for a missing region; the host refuses it in the second half of the plan. When the reference is the whole picture (the canvas's proportions), map all of it onto the whole canvas; the host lays a toned ground in its colours first, and under a time allowance widens the steps so the job fits, then says so in the result. Realism comes from your own strokes on top: paint_contours for silhouettes and overlaps, paint_pass with small brushes and long directed strokes for edges, glints and fine detail, clipped layers for light, shadow and each material. Blurring a dab texture makes it blurry, not realistic. To finish from a reference, use paint_reference_edges on focal regions (the figure, a vehicle, a railing, lit windows) with a crisp preset such as b) Basic-6 Details: it paints the reference's own edges as thin strokes along each edge, one in each side's colour, which makes contours, highlights and reflections crisp. Target the canvas region where that content already sits, then add material strokes by hand. Where dab or mosaic texture remains between edges (hair, fabric, skin, sky, water, road), use paint_reference_surface with a Color Smudge blender such as k) Blender Basic: it smudges the paint already there along the reference's form flow in the smooth areas, on its own layer, then repaints the region's edges crisp; it replaces blur, which only makes dabs blurry. Busy areas (lit windows, reflections, snow, fur) are left for paint_reference_edges and your own strokes. On a painted canvas every transfer, edges and surface included, is refused past 2x enlargement (target over source): a goal image much smaller than the canvas cannot sharpen hair or a face. Attach a closer reference of that area, or stop refining it there and ask the artist. After the underpainting a chat gets one whole-canvas softening pass (a smudge paint_pass or a blur filter over 60% of the canvas); soften later only one element or region. For a face or a hand late in the plan, attach a close-up reference of it if one exists and run paint_from_reference on just that feature (at most 3% of the canvas, so one face or hand per call), then paint_reference_edges on the same target; a focal patch is allowed at any step. paint_from_reference paints the whole job (coarse, fine and focal passes) in one call; call continue_reference_painting with its job_id only if a job was stopped or interrupted. Use list_reference_paintings to recover job IDs after restart. Choose source/target rectangles with matching aspect ratios and name focal source regions for faces/hands. A part reference (part_of) is see-through outside the one area to follow; a transfer from it paints only that area, in the place it has in the original. A crop is a close-up of bounds of the original. When the context lists close-ups (an element image or reference aligned to its place in the goal, confirmed by the artist), keep giving detail transfers their source in goal pixels: one whose source lies inside a close-up's goal box samples the sharper close-up instead, and the result says so. Until the artist confirms a close-up (they are asked beside the run), transfers there sample the goal; never wait for the answer. Close-ups that could not be placed are looks to follow, not sources. This is reference-assisted transfer, not independently drawn anatomy. For original invention or freely composed work, use authored painting tools instead.
