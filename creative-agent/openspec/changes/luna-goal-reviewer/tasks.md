Builder's notes: Luna's goal comparison plugs into the goal-likeness extension, not beside it: it runs only after ΔE has kept a batch and only where its answer could count, so exemptions (accents, finish, transfers, layer settings, departures), measure only and the "no goal underpainting" rule (`frame.protect`, which also covers element steps) apply unchanged, and its undos share the move's cap with ΔE and Luna's move review.
